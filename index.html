WARDSTONE

Your wards hold the tracks — you hold what the tracks don't cover.
Difficulty
Mana drops where things die and must be walked over.

How to play

☰
Wards hold ground. Wall a track and goblins stop and hack at the wall instead of walking round it. They never find a way around — there isn't one.
●
Will-o-wisps do not walk. They fly straight over every wall you own. One ward in four can even reach them, and it is the weakest one. The rest is your crossbow.
■
Trolls out-hit your hammer. You cannot mend a wall faster than a troll ruins it. A wall buys you time to kill it, and nothing more.
